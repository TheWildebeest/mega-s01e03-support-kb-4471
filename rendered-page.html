The problems

  1. Parser. The shared parser reads every date month first. Day-first tenants get flipped dates. This is the bug #4471 describes.
  2. Booking-v3 hint. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. People at day-first tenants (copper.studio, alder.physio, pale.harbour) type month first. This is a different bug and a different fix: the form, not the parser.
  3. One-day move. Dates land one day off, not a month flipped. The desk noted five such reports. The count by tenant files them under parser, which is wrong.

Only the first problem belongs in #4471. The other two have a ticket of their own.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges on subject line alone. Subjects like "Date is wrong" fit all three problems, and intake's browser guess (intakeTag) marked every report parser. Count causes by tenant locale and form, not by subject or tag.
